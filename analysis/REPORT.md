# Offline analysis report (synthetic stream)

Generated by `analysis/generate_orbit_trajectory.py` + `plot_orbit_analysis.py`.  
Purpose: give reviewers **data to analyze** without downloading DeepSeek weights.

## Setup

- 200 steps, 64 experts, top-6, \(d=64\)
- Ground truth: \(\mathrm{top\text{-}k}\) experts by cosine similarity of \(h\) to fixed prototypes
- Four regime shifts (every 50 steps) move the mean of \(h\)
- Baselines: `prev_copy`, `frequency`, `cyclic`
- Method: `OrbitPredictor` (deposit after each true set)

## Honest reading

This stream is **synthetic and learnable** by design (so the field dynamics are visible).  
It is **not** a substitute for live DeepSeek-V2-Lite gate-hit numbers (see `results/v36_*` for hardware-bound lab).

Open `data/orbit_trajectory_200.json` (~200KB) for per-step `true` / `orbit` / hits / `S_env` stats.
